About

Hey there, I’m Ira Phatak, the person behind The Side Quest Club. I've always had a thing for ideas. The random ones, the slightly unhinged ones, the ones that start with “wait, what if…” and somehow turn into hours of thinking, researching, or trying to figure out how to make them real.

The problem? Having ideas is easy. Actually doing something with them is a whole different side quest.

So, I made this.

The Side Quest Club is my little corner of the internet where curiosity turns into action. It’s where I experiment with things, learn skills, build projects, chase strange ideas, and document the process — including the parts where absolutely nothing works.

Right now, that means coding. I’m in my first year of computer engineering at Cummins College of Engineering in Karvenagar, so a lot of this is me learning as I go — lectures during the day, side quests after. Tomorrow, it could mean something completely different. I’m not here to stick to one thing, have everything figured out, or wait until I’m good enough to begin. I’m here to try things, get things wrong, learn something new, and see where it takes me.

And eventually, I want this to become more than just a place to collect my own projects. I want it to be a space where curious people can find each other, share what they’re making, discover new interests, and start side quests of their own.

Because why should we have to wait for the perfect idea, the right time, or a five-year plan to do something interesting?

This is me finally putting my ideas into action. One side quest at a time. You’re welcome to tag along. Who knows what we’ll end up making?
